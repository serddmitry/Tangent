New since v0.12.3-dima.9:

Quick Open
- A note now keeps its place in the most-recently-opened ordering after it's renamed. // Creating a note and giving it a title renames its file, which used to orphan the note's recency — so a note you'd just made and opened would sink below notes you'd opened much earlier instead of staying near the top of the Cmd+O list.
